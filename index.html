Pulse IPTV – Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · Applies to Pulse IPTV for Android, Android TV / Google TV, iPhone, iPad and Apple TV · Developer: Mohamed Elsisi

In short: Pulse IPTV is a video player. It does not include any channels or content, shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking, and never sells your data. Your data stays on your device unless you turn on the optional Pulse Sync, which stores it in your own sync account so your other devices can use it.

1. What Pulse IPTV is

Pulse IPTV plays streams from an IPTV service that you subscribe to separately (for example an Xtream Codes compatible provider). The developer does not provide, sell, host or control any channels, movies, series or other content and has no relationship with any provider. A built-in demo uses openly licensed sample videos.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Your IPTV account details: server address, username and password.
  • Favourites, Continue Watching progress, recently watched channels and your settings.
  • Saved movie information and IMDb ratings (a cache), and your OMDb key if you add one.
  • If the app closes unexpectedly, a short technical report so you can copy it and send it yourself. It is never sent automatically.
  • Videos you choose to download.

This data is removed when you delete the profile in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

All connections the app makes to Firebase and OMDb are encrypted (HTTPS). Connections to your IPTV provider use whatever address your provider gives you; many providers use plain HTTP.

4. Pulse Sync (optional)

Pulse Sync keeps your phone, tablet and TVs in step. If you sign in with Google, the following is stored in Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), linked to your sync account:

  • Your Google account's email address and a user ID.
  • Your IPTV accounts (name, server, username, password and OMDb key), so a new device can sign in by itself.
  • Favourites and watch progress for each IPTV account.
  • The names of TVs you link to your account.

This data is used only to sync your own devices. It is not shared with anyone else, not used for advertising and not analysed. It is encrypted in transit and stored by Google as the developer's service provider (Firebase privacy information). It is kept until you delete it (section 5).

5. Deleting your data

Delete your Pulse Sync account and data:

  • In the app: Settings › Sync › Delete my sync data (on the phone or tablet you signed in on). This deletes your stored IPTV accounts, favourites, watch progress, linked TVs and the sync account itself, straight away.
  • Without the app: email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the Google account you used, with the subject "Delete Pulse Sync data". Everything is deleted within 30 days and you get a confirmation.

Data on your device: delete the profile in the app, or uninstall the app.

6. What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, tracking or profiling.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access. (The camera is only used, on your request, by Google Play services' code scanner to link a TV, and nothing is saved.)

7. Children

Pulse IPTV is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. A parental PIN can hide adult categories.
